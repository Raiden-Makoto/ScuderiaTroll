1. Wheel Count, Function & Swappability (3 to 6 Wheels): Cars must run 3, 4, 5, or 6 wheels. Every wheel must actively contribute to steering or stabilization and must be externally accessible and swappable during a pit stop (banning hidden retractable "landing gear" wheels).
2. Ground Contact ("Airtime" Clause): At least one wheel must remain in contact with the track at all times. The only exception is brief, unintentional airtime caused by track bumps, kerbs, or crests; no system may be designed to lift the car off the track.
3. Dimensional Box: The car, including all bodywork and aerodynamic devices in any position, must fit within a box of 5.5 m length × 2.0 m width × 1.2 m height. Wheelbase is measured between the centrelines of the foremost and rearmost axles, and is limited as follows:
   - Two-axle cars (including 2+4 or 4+2 dually layouts, with paired wheels side by side on one axle): 3.6 m.
   - Three-axle cars (e.g. 2-2-2, 2-1-2, or any permutation thereof), classified by the position of the middle axle's centreline, measured from the foremost axle's centreline as a percentage of the total wheelbase (overhangs excluded):
     - (a) Middle axle within 35%–65% of total wheelbase: 1.8 m between any two adjacent axles.
     - (b) Middle axle outside that range (axles paired at the front or rear): 3.0 m total wheelbase.
4. Open Clean Propulsion & Zero Hazardous Materials (No Pure-Electric Cars): Any powertrain architecture is permitted, provided the car is not powered solely by energy stored in batteries, capacitors, or other onboard electrical storage. At least part of the car's propulsive energy must come from an alternative fuel carried on board, meaning any fuel other than conventional fossil petrol or diesel (e.g. renewable diesel/HVO, e-fuels, bio-ethanol, hydrogen, LPG, CNG). There is no minimum share, but the fuel-powered system must genuinely contribute to propulsion during the race, either by driving the wheels directly or by generating electricity used for propulsion (e.g. a range-extender engine or fuel cell); a fuel system carried as dead weight or ballast does not qualify. Power delivery must respond to the driver's throttle pedal and the car's operating state only: driver-activated temporary boosts (e.g. push-to-pass or "overtake" buttons) and deployment modes triggered by proximity to another car are prohibited. Power unit maps and modes are fixed at the start of the race and may only be changed under Safety Car or in the pit lane; each change counts as a pit stop under the Sporting Rules. The powertrain must emit zero harmful exhaust gases, where exhaust is considered harmless if it stays below the U.S. EPA Tier 4 line-haul locomotive emission limits (NOx 1.3, PM 0.03, HC 0.14, CO 1.5 g/bhp-hr), averaged over the whole race; CO2 and water vapour are not counted as harmful. Radioactive materials (including depleted uranium ballast) and unsafe chemical reactions are prohibited anywhere on the car.
5. Open Global Materials (No Export-Restricted / Exclusive Alloys): Every material used in the car's construction must be available to all competing teams worldwide without national export bans, defense classifications, or single-manufacturer exclusivity. Exotic or newly developed compounds (e.g. a novel alloy stronger and lighter than titanium) are permitted, provided they can be produced in a standard laboratory or factory without rare or highly specialized equipment. Equipment commonly found in major engine or automotive manufacturers' factories, such as large-format metal 3D printers, counts as standard.
6. Cockpit Canopy Freedom & Default Safety/Weight: Teams may build either an open cockpit (with Halo protection) or a closed fighter-jet style canopy meeting FIA rapid-extraction safety standards. Standard FIA survival cells, crash structures, fire suppression, and minimum weight rules remain mandatory. Minimum weight is measured without fuel or exhaust aftertreatment fluid (DEF/urea).
7. Total Design Freedom: Everything not restricted by Rules 1–6 (active aero, fan cars, active suspension, custom layouts) is 100% unrestricted.
